The question that started this

What happens
beyond engineering?

Andrew Ng’s AI engineering skills map, presented at BayLearn, inspired a broader question: how are AI-related expectations taking shape in user research, design, product management, and marketing?

Changing Work follows that question through job ads. The aim is to help students, practitioners, and educators see the specific capabilities employers are asking for, examine the evidence, and distinguish established expectations from emerging possibilities.

The software-engineering hierarchy follows Ng’s published categories. This study adds its own operational definitions, documented occupational samples, coding review, and a plan for tracking change. It is an independent project; the percentages here come from our samples.

02 / How the study works

The method behind the map.

Built to make both the findings and their limits easy to examine.

This exploratory study examines public job descriptions from a bounded source. It keeps the original occupational samples distinct, records the coding rules, and links interpretations to source evidence.

    What this can tell us

    Which defined AI-related tasks and capabilities appear in these sampled job ads, how they are described, and where occupational expectations overlap.
